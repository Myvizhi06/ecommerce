## 🛍️ Key Features

1. **Dual Buying & Selling Functionality**:
   - **For Buyers**: Browse items, filter by categories, live search, sort by price/rating, wishlist items, and buy with escrow simulation.
   - **For Sellers**: Click **"Sell Item"** to list your own product for sale with image uploads (file or URL), title, category, condition, price, and description.
2. **Instant Marketplace Listing**:
   - Newly created listings appear immediately at the top of the marketplace with a distinctive **"My Listing"** badge.
3. **Interactive Shopping Cart Drawer**:
   - Slide-in side drawer with quantity adjusters (`+` / `-`), auto calculated taxes, subtotal, and free shipping over $50.
4. **Checkout & Escrow Protection Modal**:
   - Secure checkout form with generated randomized Escrow Order ID and confirmation.
5. **Data Persistence**:
   - Uses `localStorage` so your published listings, cart items, and wishlist persist across browser refreshes.
6. **Modern Responsive Design**:
   - Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens with smooth animations and toasts.
